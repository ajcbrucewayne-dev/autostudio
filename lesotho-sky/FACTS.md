# Facts used in "Kingdom in the Sky"

| Claim | Basis |
|---|---|
| Lesotho's lowest point is ~1,400 m, the highest lowest point of any country | CIA World Factbook (via IndexMundi): lowest point "junction of the Orange and Makhaleng Rivers 1,400 m"; Wikipedia, Makhaleng River: "the highest lowest point of any country". Our terrain data measures ~1,395 m (data/measure.json) |
| Britain's highest mountain, Ben Nevis, is 1,345 m | Ordnance Survey 2016 remeasurement (1,344.527 m → 1,345 m); Wikipedia "Ben Nevis" |
| Lesotho is completely surrounded by South Africa | Geography of Lesotho (Wikipedia): enclave; "completely surrounded by South Africa from 1910" |
| Sea +1,000 m: ~43 % of South Africa underwater; +1,400 m: ~79 % ("about four-fifths") | Measured: AWS Terrain Tiles z8 (~600 m) over Natural Earth 1:10m South Africa polygon, area-weighted share below each level (tools/prep.py, data/measure.json). Hydrologically naive (inland basins counted as flooded) |
| Peak "tops three thousand four hundred metres"; summit 3,482 m (Thabana Ntlenyana) | CIA World Factbook; Wikipedia "Thabana Ntlenyana" |
| 1868: the king (Moshoeshoe I) asked Britain for protection from the Boers (Orange Free State) | SAHO, "The three Basotho Wars (1858–68)": "On the 12 March 1868, the British parliament declared the Basotho Kingdom a British protectorate"; Moshoeshoe sought British protection after the Free State overran most of his land |
| Independence 1966 | Wikipedia "Basutoland": independent as Lesotho on 4 October 1966 |
| "Kingdom in the Sky" | Widely used nickname, incl. the Government of Lesotho (gov.ls) |
