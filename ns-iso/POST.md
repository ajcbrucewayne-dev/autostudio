# Off The Map — North Sentinel Island (isometric)

**Title:** The Island Nobody Is Allowed to Visit 🏝️🚫 #shorts

**Description:**
There's one island on Earth you're not allowed to set foot on, and the people living there want it that way.
North Sentinel Island in India's Andaman Islands is home to the Sentinelese, one of the last uncontacted peoples on Earth. After 1880, when British officers reportedly took some of them away, they have met outsiders with arrows, including a helicopter after the 2004 tsunami. Today, India protects the island: boats must keep their distance, and visiting is illegal. Not just for our safety, but for theirs.

Would you respect their choice? 👇
