# Off The Map — Svalbard Global Seed Vault (isometric)

**Title:** The Doomsday Vault Has Already Been Opened 🧊🌱 #shorts

**Description:**
Inside an Arctic mountain, there's a door built for the end of the world, and someone has already opened it.
The Svalbard Global Seed Vault in Norway holds more than a million seed samples from almost every country, kept at −18 °C deep inside the permafrost. In 2015, war in Syria cut scientists off from their seed bank in Aleppo, so for the first time they withdrew seeds from the vault, regrew them in Lebanon and Morocco, and later sent fresh seeds back.

What would YOU save inside? 👇
